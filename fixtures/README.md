# Test fixture inventory

Run `scripts/build-fixtures.sh` with c2patool 0.26.60, ExifTool 13.55, and ffmpeg to rebuild
the generated fixtures. The script records every fixture hash in `SHA256SUMS`.

## Sources and licenses

- `official/`: unmodified files from the C2PA public test files repository at revision
  `22beccc075707475b038d8789d0136c009e43143`. License: CC BY-SA. Source:
  <https://spec.c2pa.org/public-testfiles/>.
- All other image fixtures are generated from a plain white image by `build-fixtures.sh`.
  They are original test data for Sourceglass and are covered by the repository's MIT License.
- The malformed fixtures are generated locally and contain no third-party material.

The official files are copied byte-for-byte. No fixture is resized or re-saved after its target
metadata is written.

`broken-huge-exif.jpg` is kept just above the metadata limit, while
`xmp-large-within-limit.jpg` is kept just below it. The earlier 13.3 MB performance fixture is not
stored in the repository; its measurements remain in the Phase 2 measurement record.

Fixtures under `real/` are intentionally not generated by this script. They require outputs whose
service terms permit redistribution and are structural snapshots rather than deterministic verdict
fixtures.
